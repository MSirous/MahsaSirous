👋 Hi there

My name is Mahsa Sirous. I am a software engineer and early-career AI
researcher based in Singapore. My interests lie at the intersection of
**deep learning** and **reinforcement learning**, with a strong focus on
theoretical foundations — understanding *why* and *how* models work, not
just how to use them.

This shows in my work: I build methods from scratch, neural networks, and
the full RL stack from bandits to policy gradients to ground myself in the
underlying mathematics. My current direction is **Trust-Aware Multi-Agent
LLM Systems with Reinforcement Learning**: how a system can learn which
agents to rely on, building on prior research in reinforcement learning and
recommender systems.

At this page I share my open-source projects and notes from my learning and
research, hoping to exchange ideas and grow together with everyone!
